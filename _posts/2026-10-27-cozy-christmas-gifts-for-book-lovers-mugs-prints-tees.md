## A few gifting tips

- **Order early.** Everything is printed when you order it, which takes a few extra days, and holiday shipping gets busy. Ordering in November or the first half of December is the safest bet.
- **Ship it straight to them.** You can enter the recipient's address at checkout and send the gift directly.
- **Make a little bundle.** A mug, a bag of their favorite tea or cocoa and a bookmark makes a complete gift for under $25.
- **Not sure about shirt sizes?** Our tees are a classic unisex fit. When in doubt, go one size up for a relaxed fit.
