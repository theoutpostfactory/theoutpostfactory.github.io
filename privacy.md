# Privacy policy

_Last updated: October 25, 2026_

The Outpost Factory ("we") runs this blog, the Etsy shop [The Outpost Factory](https://www.etsy.com/shop/TheOutpostFactory), and a small internal tool called the Outpost Station that helps us manage our own shop.

## This blog
This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. We don't use cookies, ads, analytics or sign-ups, and we don't collect personal information from visitors. GitHub may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses) as described in [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). Links to our Etsy shop take you to Etsy, which has its own privacy policy.

## Orders
Purchases happen on Etsy and are printed and shipped by our print-on-demand partner. Your order details are handled by Etsy and that partner under their privacy policies. Our tool reads order totals and statuses to track our sales; it does not store customer names or addresses.

## Our Pinterest app (Outpost Station)
The Outpost Station connects only to The Outpost Factory's own Pinterest account. It uses Pinterest's API to read our own boards and to create Pins for our own products and blog posts. It does not access other people's Pins, boards or personal data. The sign-in token Pinterest gives us is stored only on our own computer and is never shared or sold. You can contact us to ask about this at any time.
